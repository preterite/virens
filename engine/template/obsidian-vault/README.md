# VIRENS Obsidian Vault

## Structure

- `000_inbox/` - Quick capture and processing
- `100_daily/` - Daily notes
- `200_readings/` - Literature notes and annotations
  - `automated_annotation_imports/` - Raw exports from Highlights, Readwise
  - `distilled_literature_summaries/` - Cleaned, processed literature notes
  - `readwise_imports/` - Automatic Readwise highlight imports
  - `rough_reading_sessions/` - Quick notes during reading sessions
- `300_zettels/` - Permanent notes (atomic ideas in your own words)
- `400_domain/` - Domain knowledge and Maps of Content (MOCs)
- `500_projects/` - Active research projects
- `600_teaching/` - Course materials and teaching notes
- `700_lab/` - Experiments, drafts, and works-in-progress
- `800_outputs/` - Completed work ready for publication
- `900_meta/` - Vault documentation and workflows

## Workflow

1. **Capture** in `000_inbox/` - Quick entry point for all fleeting thoughts
2. **Process daily** in `100_daily/` - Daily notes with reflections and planning
3. **Create literature notes** in `200_readings/` - One note per source
4. **Distill to permanent notes** in `300_zettels/` - Atomic, evergreen ideas
5. **Organize with MOCs** in `400_domain/` - Connect ideas thematically
6. **Work on projects** in `500_projects/` - Combine notes into manuscripts
7. **Publish from** `800_outputs/` - Final, polished work

## Note Types

**Literature Notes** (`200_readings/distilled_literature_summaries/`)
- One note per source (book, article, etc.)
- Filename: `@authorYEAR-shortkey.md`
- Contains: Key ideas, quotes with page numbers, your commentary
- Links to: Bookends reference, DEVONthink PDF, related Zettels

**Permanent Notes / Zettels** (`300_zettels/`)
- One idea per note
- Filename: Descriptive title (e.g., `Writing as Process.md`)
- Written in your own words
- Links to: Literature notes (evidence), other Zettels (connections)

**Maps of Content** (`400_domain/`)
- Thematic hub notes
- Connect related Zettels and literature notes
- Evolve as your understanding develops
- Examples: `Digital Rhetoric MOC.md`, `Composition Theory MOC.md`

## Git Note

This vault structure is tracked in your private user repository. Your actual research notes are your private data and never committed to the public VIRENS framework.
